Prof. Khairul Anam is a Professor of Electrical Engineering at Universitas Jember (UNEJ), Indonesia. He leads the Intelligent System and Robotics Laboratory (ISR Lab) at CDAST-UNEJ, where his team develops cutting-edge solutions at the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, and biomedical engineering.

His research focuses on two main pillars: Healthcare Robotics (prosthetic hands, stroke rehabilitation robots, smart wheelchairs) and AI for Agriculture & Health (intelligent coffee dryers, lung cancer detection via X-ray deep learning).

He actively mentors undergraduate (S1), master's (S2), and doctoral (S3) students in Electrical Engineering, guiding them from fundamental research to real-world application and publication.
